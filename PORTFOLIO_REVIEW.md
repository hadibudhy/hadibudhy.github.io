One is the decorative MDS teaser; the other 97 are chart or diagram assets. Three source-backed legacy analyses are now published as Next.js case studies. Two additional cases use official EIA and U.S. Department of Education data. The MDS article remains a draft because its linked Databricks tables are not available in the repository.

## Data corrections and content changes

- **Airbnb marketplace:** re-derived top-host concentration from 16,474 observed host IDs. The top 165 hosts hold 7,566 of 30,555 listings, or 24.8%. The room-type split and 97.6% combined share were asserted against the June 2026 snapshot.
- **World happiness:** verified the balanced 129-country panel against the 2026 workbook. Corrected the social-support group gap to 0.83 points, social-support correlation to 0.70, and generosity correlation to 0.04.
- **NYC taxi operations:** corrected the chart filter to January 2025 pickups with positive trip distance and total amount and durations from 1 to 120 minutes. The checks reproduce 3,312,701 trips, 241,585 at 18:00, 2,962,526 Manhattan pickups, and 133,337 JFK pickups. Corrected JFK total amount from $81.11 to $81.10 and clarified that the amount is not driver profit.
- **Airfare pricing:** updated the comparison to official 2024 and 2025 BTS annual releases: current-dollar fares of $384 and $387, and the 2024 inflation-adjusted comparison of $394. The article notes the sampling change in the 2025 annual result.
- **Netflix content strategy:** restored the 8,807-row source snapshot locally from Zenodo, checked its MD5 against the preserved record, and re-derived the same-year acquisition trend, movie-cluster changes, and UK release-vintage metadata rates. Corrected the article to describe the cluster labels and sparse fields actually measured.
- **Fake Job detection:** restored the 17,880-row EMSCAD source snapshot locally and reran the model pipeline with the original batch size. The stratified holdout contains 3,576 posts, including 173 fraudulent posts. At a 0.10 threshold, the combined model reached 75.1% recall, 98% precision, and 0.850 F1. The five-fold F1 mean was 0.8251 with a 0.0208 standard deviation. Four article charts were regenerated from this run.
- **Commercial electricity:** used 5,208 EIA monthly records from 2019 through 2025. The sales-weighted U.S. commercial price rose from 10.68 to 13.41 cents per kilowatt-hour in nominal terms. State price and growth patterns differ; no company bill or savings were estimated.
- **College Scorecard:** collapsed repeated branch rows from 227,980 source rows to 217,369 distinct institution-system, field, and credential records. For domestic bachelor's records, 25,283 of 67,212 reported a four-year earnings median. Coverage varied by institution control, so the article treats the measure as a review signal rather than a ranking.
- **Legacy publication:** the Netflix, Happiness, and Fake Job articles now have Problem, Hypotheses, Analysis, and Result sections and published evidence manifests. Their archived Jekyll copies remain in the repository. The MDS post stays a draft because it requires source tables from a Databricks workspace that is not available here.
- Story text was updated for Netflix, Happiness, Fake Job, commercial electricity, and College Scorecard. Other article copy was not changed in this revision.
