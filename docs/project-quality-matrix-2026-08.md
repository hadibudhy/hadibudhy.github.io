## Classification decision

- **Flagship:** four completed or implemented cases with the clearest decision ownership and evidence boundary.
- **Strong completed case:** computed public-data analysis with specific findings and validation, but no professional outcome claim.
- **Methods / design study:** useful source-grounded analytical design where this repository does not contain a checked-in dataset and runnable project-specific computation. These pages use “What the source supports” and “Evidence and design visuals” rather than empty “Key findings” and implied empirical output.

No project was removed. The 14 methods/design studies remain available because they add distinct coverage in market screening, product measurement, operations, data modeling, or applied AI while their evidence limits are now visible at card and page level.
