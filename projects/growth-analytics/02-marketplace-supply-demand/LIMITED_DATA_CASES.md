# Limited-data marketplace cases

These are interview drills, not observed TLC findings. Start by clarifying the unit, time window, and missing denominators before recommending an intervention.

| Case | Given numbers | Ideal reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Excess demand | Requests +20%, completed rides +8%, wait +18% | Supply is not keeping up; verify driver hours and cancellations. |
| 2 Excess supply | Requests -10%, active drivers +15%, utilization -22% | Supply may be ahead of demand; avoid adding incentives. |
| 3 Poor matching | Requests flat, trips flat, wait +25% | Suspect geography or dispatch friction; trips alone cannot identify it. |
| 4 Price pressure | Fare +12%, rides -9%, revenue +2% | Revenue rose but volume fell; check contribution and retention. |
| 5 Incentive lift | Incentive cost +30%, rides +5%, driver hours +28% | Test incremental rides per driver-hour, not gross rides. |
| 6 Zone imbalance | Zone A wait +30%, Zone B wait -10%, city wait flat | Problem is local; test rebalancing and watch displacement. |
| 7 Airport demand | Airport trips +15%, duration +20%, driver pay +8% | Airport lane grew but productivity must be measured per driver-hour. |
| 8 Weekend peak | Saturday trips +40%, active drivers +12%, wait +16% | Peak supply gap is plausible; compare hourly windows and weather. |
| 9 Driver productivity | Trips +6%, driver-hours +18%, earnings +4% | More supply produced lower productivity; investigate demand mix. |
| 10 Declining conversion | Requests flat, pickups -12%, cancellations +20% | Diagnose fulfilment and supply before changing price. |
| 11 Spillover | Treatment-zone trips +9%, neighboring-zone trips -8% | Incentive may have moved drivers rather than added capacity. |
| 12 Fee change | CBD fee +$1.50, fare +5%, rides -3% | Separate fee pass-through from demand response; use a control area. |
| 13 Provider mix | Provider A trips +15%, Provider B -4%, city total +3% | Mix shifted; do not infer market growth from one provider. |
| 14 Weather shock | Rain-day wait +35%, trips +2%, driver hours -9% | Weather may explain pressure; compare similar non-rain days. |
| 15 Low-volume zone | Trips -20%, wait unknown, requests unknown | Evidence is insufficient; collect request, wait, and supply measures. |
