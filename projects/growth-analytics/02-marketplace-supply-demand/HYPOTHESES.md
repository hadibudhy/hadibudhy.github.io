# Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Evidence needed | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Demand is concentrated in predictable hourly windows | Trip counts by hour and zone | Concentrate supply or incentives in those windows |
| Completed trips understate unmet demand | Compare trip counts with wait, cancellation, and request data | Do not call trip volume total demand without those fields |
| Airport flows are structurally different | Origin, destination, duration, and time-of-day mix | Treat airport service as a separate operating lane |
| A citywide incentive can move drivers rather than add supply | Zone-level displacement and driver-hour data | Prefer geographic or switchback tests |
| Low volume may reflect low demand or poor supply | Need requests, fulfillment, and wait time together | Do not target a zone from trips alone |
