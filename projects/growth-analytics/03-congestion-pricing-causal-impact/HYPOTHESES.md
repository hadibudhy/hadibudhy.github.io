# Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Outcome | Design check | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Congestion pricing changes CBD trip volume | Trips per zone-hour | Event study and placebo dates | Adjust CBD pricing or supply only if the effect survives controls |
| Effects differ inside and outside the zone | Trips and fares | Difference-in-differences with matched control zones | Target geography rather than the whole city |
| Fee pass-through changes passenger fare | Fare and fee | Fee field plus pre-period baseline | Monitor conversion and driver pay together |
| Airports and through-trips respond differently | Airport/CBD flow | Route and origin segmentation | Keep airport strategy separate |
| A short pre/post window can mislead | All outcomes | Longer historical pre-trend and placebo tests | Report no causal result when assumptions fail |
